# Availability

Statuses: `AVAILABLE_NOW`, `AVAILABLE_TODAY`, `AVAILABLE_LATER`, `LIMITED`, `BUSY`, `NOT_TODAY`, `UNKNOWN`.

`AVAILABLE_NOW` expires after four hours. `AVAILABLE_TODAY` expires at the end of the business country timezone. Public copy includes freshness: “Available now — confirmed 14 mins ago.”

Professionals use **I WANT WORK** on `/professional` or a signed email token at `/action/availability`. Listings without a live trial or paid subscription, and suspended businesses, cannot turn Available Now on.
